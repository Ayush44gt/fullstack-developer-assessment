## 2. Without the profiler (view called directly, median of 5 runs)

Called through DRF's `APIRequestFactory` with `force_authenticate`, so there are no session/user queries.

| Customer | Orders | Endpoint | SQL queries | Local SQLite (ms) | With 2 ms DB round trip (ms) |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| light_customer | 5 | regressed | **36** | 8.4 | 126.9 |
| light_customer | 5 | fixed | **2** | 3.0 | 9.1 |
| heavy_customer | 250 | regressed | **1611** | 346.0 | 6378.7 |
| heavy_customer | 250 | fixed | **2** | 23.8 | 27.2 |

In-process SQLite has no network hop, which hides most of the cost. The last column adds a 2 ms sleep per query, roughly one round trip to a managed Postgres in the same region. Production adds more: connection-pool waits and a busy database while every heavy customer refreshes the dashboard. At 15–20 ms per query under that load, ~1,600 queries is 24–32 s: the 30 s worker timeout in the incident.
